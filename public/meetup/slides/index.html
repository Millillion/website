1. Deliberation and specification: decide exactly what you want, with the AI's help. 2. Autonomous implementation: the AI does the work. 3. Checks: human cognitive stochastic checks (you read it), AI cognitive stochastic checks (other agents review it), and deterministic checks (tests, type checks, linters, proofs: same answer every time). 4. The AI presents evidence; you judge it. If it's not right yet, back to implementation. This works for any knowledge work, not just software.

Robust verification combines deterministic and stochastic checks. Deterministic checks are repeatable and predictable: always the same result for the same input. Usually a program or script: tests, type checks, linters, proofs. Stochastic checks are what AIs and humans do: reading, judging, reviewing. Useful, but results can vary. Order: get as many deterministic checks as possible. Whatever can't be done deterministically, check stochastically with AI, as much as possible. Whatever is left over must be checked by humans. The agentic engineer guides the AI through this: fill up the deterministic checks first, then decide what AI review can cover, then what a human must review. Formal verification is the strongest deterministic check: a mathematical proof that the code is correct.

AI's abilities are jagged: far better than people at some things, surprisingly bad at others. Peaks: writing, translation, coding, summarizing, recalling facts across every field. Valleys (examples to choose from): local knowledge and relationships, judgment and taste, trust and accountability, physical work, long tasks with unclear goals, knowing what's worth doing. The valleys are our opportunity, if we build those skills. But each new model raises the line, and the valleys keep shrinking. How long they last is the open question.

The job shifts from doing the work to judging the evidence the AI presents. Let the AI do more and more, and present you with evidence. That's how one person scales. It applies to any knowledge work: a contract, a drawing, a budget, a report.
