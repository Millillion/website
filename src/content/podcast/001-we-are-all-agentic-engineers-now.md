The first episode of the Millillion podcast.

Jordan Last (lastmjs) tells the story of how AI went from chatbots that made up CLI commands to agents that can generate all of the code, and why that makes all of us **agentic engineers** now, with an emphasis in our own area of expertise.

- From GPT-3 and ChatGPT to the first coding agents in late 2024
- The jump in late 2025, when agents started writing all the code
- What an agentic engineer does: managing agents, judging the evidence they present, and building a pipeline that refines slop into clean code
- Jagged intelligence, and why human judgment still matters for now
- Mentoring a new developer who learned to code in the AI era
